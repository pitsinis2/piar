    1. Project structure

    Projects contain floors, areas, service teams, plans, files, photos, notes, chat, archive, and audit history.

    Workers open a project, select the correct area, and upload photos, files, or notes from phone or PC.

    The company owner and project manager can see all areas, assigned teams, archived elements, planner, clients, equipment, and reports.

    The production version adds real login, company licenses, Supabase database, Google Drive archive, and automatic backup/sync.
